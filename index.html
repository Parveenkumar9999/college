Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

Which college online work can you help with?

Reappear forms, anti-ragging forms, exam forms, fee payment, scholarship forms, admission forms, applications, result and exam information, migration/certificate application guidance, and other college online work.

How can I contact you?

WhatsApp or call 8506077343, or submit the enquiry form on this page.

How do I submit my enquiry?

Fill in the enquiry form and tap "Send Enquiry on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with your details ready; tap Send to deliver it.

Can you help with reappear forms?

Yes, we can guide you through the reappear/back-paper form procedure and the details you need. Final acceptance is decided by your college or university.

Can you help with anti-ragging forms?

Yes, we can guide you through the online anti-ragging declaration process.

Can you help with online fee payment?

Yes, we can help you understand the online payment steps and the details required. Payments go only through your college's official portal.

What information should I provide?

Your name, mobile number, college, course, year/semester, the service you need, and a short message describing your requirement.

Is this an official college/university website?

No. This is an independent student assistance service.
